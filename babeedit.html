The Babe Edit

A hand-coded editorial site exploring the marketing campaigns that redefined beauty, fashion, and self-expression — built as a final project for a web design course, and driven by a genuine interest in the strategy behind iconic brand moments.

Role

About the Project

The Babe Edit is a curated collection of the marketing campaigns that didn't just sell products, they sold confidence, community, and realness. Featuring breakdowns of Aerie Real, Rare Beauty, Barbie, and Glossier, the site explores what makes a campaign feel iconic, from body-positive messaging to color-driven cultural moments. Built and coded entirely from scratch, the project pairs an editorial, magazine-style tone with clean layout and typography.

Featured Breakdowns

Aerie's #AerieREAL, Rare Beauty's #BeRare, Barbie's global #PinkMovement, and Glossier's emotion-driven brand storytelling — each analyzed through the lens of what made the campaign resonate and endure.
